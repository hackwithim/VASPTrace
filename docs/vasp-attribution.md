# VASP Attribution Engine & Intelligence Methodology

## 1. Principles of Law-Enforcement Attribution

In compliance with financial crime regulations and judicial standards:
1. **Intelligence, Not Automatic Legal Proof**: All attributions are presented as investigative intelligence signals.
2. **Deterministic Priority**: Direct matches with known custodial deposit addresses take strict priority over heuristic clustering.
3. **Never State Absolute Ownership**: Results document that transaction flow reaches a known VASP-controlled address, not that the suspect owns the entire entity.
4. **Mandatory Human Review**: No freezing orders or freezing notices are dispatched automatically without officer certification.

---

## 2. Attribution Status Hierarchy

| Status Tier | Definition | Confidence Signal | Actionable Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`EVIDENCE_BACKED`** | Direct transaction into a verified VASP deposit or hot address | 90% - 99% | Court-ready preservation request; SAHYOG draft ready |
| **`STRONG_CANDIDATE`** | Multi-hop path into verified VASP cluster infrastructure | 80% - 89% | Targeted intelligence inquiry with compliance team |
| **`POSSIBLE_MATCH`** | Heuristic proximity or distant cluster association | 50% - 79% | Further chain-analysis recommended |
| **`UNKNOWN`** | No known VASP deposit address reached within max hops | 0% | Search depth exhaustion; no attribution claimed |

---

## 3. Statutory Disclaimers

Every report and attribution panel prominently incorporates the statutory notice:

> *"Blockchain intelligence supports investigative analysis and does not by itself establish legal ownership or criminal liability. Attribution results should be reviewed against authoritative records and applicable legal processes."*
